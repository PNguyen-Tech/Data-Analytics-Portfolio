## 🎬 1. Pixar Financial Performance Analysis Dashboard

**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Academic Context:** Associated with University of Houston, C.T. Bauer College of Business  
**Timeline:** Jul 2026 – Aug 2026  

### **Project Overview**
This project delivers a comprehensive financial evaluation of Pixar’s complete feature film catalog. By performing robust relational data modeling and authoring custom DAX calculations, the interactive dashboard evaluates production budgets, global box office gross distributions, return on investment (ROI), critical award recognitions, and subgenre dynamics.

### **Key Practical Applications & Insights**
* **Blockbuster Dependency vs. Portfolio Stability:** Pixar’s financial performance ($13B net profit on a $4B total budget) is anchored by top-tier franchise sequels (*Toy Story*, *The Incredibles*) and select original masterworks (*Toy Story*, *Finding Nemo*, *Inside Out 2*) achieving Green-tier ROI status (>100%).
* **Genre Profitability Concentration:** While Family and Adventure films drive the highest aggregate box office volume, niche narrative categories such as Teen Drama deliver exceptional average gross yields when executed successfully.
* **Critical & Commercial Alignment:** Multi-variable correlation analysis reveals a strong positive relationship between major critical accolades (Academy Award wins) and high-tier global box office yields.

### **Dashboard Architecture & Visual Views**
* **Financial Performance & Movie Reviews:** Focuses on fiscal accounting, individual movie profitability, production budget tracking, worldwide box office grosses, ROI metrics, and critical award counts.
* **Genre Trends & Subgenre Dynamics:** Tracks box office trajectories over time, genre performance distributions, scatter-plot profitability matrices, and high-grossing subgenre breakdowns.
* **Custom Tooltip Functionality:** Context-aware interactive hover tooltips providing granular per-film metrics on demand.

## ⚡ 2. Pokémon: A Data-Driven Analysis of Media Dominance & Game Evolution

**Tool:** Tableau Desktop  
**Focus Areas:** Time-Series Analysis, Market Sentiment, Game Balance Mechanics  

### **Project Overview**
An exploratory data analysis examining Pokémon’s global media penetration alongside the statistical evolution of its core video game franchise across seven generations. This project synthesizes competitive base stats (Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, Speed) and franchise commercial performance into an interactive narrative.

### **Key Features & Visualizations**
* **Brand Recognition & Market Sentiment:** Evaluates market survey data to gauge aided brand awareness and media buzz across key demographics using comparative pie charts.
* **Video Game Sales Performance:** Tracks historical unit sales across Nintendo consoles using a time-series lollipop chart and horizontal title performance rankings.
* **Trading Card Market Economics:** Visualizes exponential physical card production trends alongside high-end secondary market valuations for rare cards.
* **Generation Type Distribution & Balance:** Interactive packed bubble and bar charts breaking down elemental type distributions and legendary counts to analyze generational power creep and meta rebalancing (e.g., introducing Dark/Steel to counter Psychic, and Fairy to counter Dragon).
